## Universal Generation Directives

* **Artistic Style:** Masterpiece Renaissance allegorical painting & illuminated codex emblem in the majestic style of Raphael, Leonardo da Vinci, and Michelangelo.
* **Composition:** Singular iconic allegorical emblem / metaphor at the central focal point, clean atmospheric negative space, high silhouette contrast.
* **Color Palette:** Deep lapis lazuli azure (`#1E3A8A`), warm Venetian gold (`#D97706`), terracotta crimson (`#DC2626`), sage emerald (`#059669`), imperial violet (`#6B21A8`), aged parchment, and chiaroscuro lighting.
* **Texture & Material:** Volumetric natural illumination, classical marble plinths, delicate Renaissance fresco plaster texture, and fine antique craquelure.
* **Recommended Aspect Ratio:** `1:1` for cards/avatars, `16:9` for wide banners.
* **Universal Negative Prompt:**
